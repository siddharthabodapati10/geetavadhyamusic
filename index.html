ABOUT YOUR TEACHER

Divya Meena Guhan

Divya Meena is a third-generation Carnatic musician who began her vocal training with her grandfather, a Veena vidwan. Her father, Sri N.S. Kalyana Raman, is an 'A' graded Mridangam artist with All India Radio Vijayawada.

She studied under Vidwan Ampolu Murali Krishna garu and Vidwan Pemmaraju Suryarao garu in Vijayawada. After earning an engineering degree, she pursued an advanced three-year course in Carnatic vocal music at the Madras Music Academy, and also completed a diploma in Carnatic Music from Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University.

🏵 Awarded the "Sangeeta Vidwath Bhooshan"
"My vision is to teach and share my knowledge with every classical music enthusiast. With ten years of expertise and participation in cultural events across the world, I aim to inspire and educate others in the world of classical music."

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

We teach traditional Carnatic vocal music, from foundational Sarali Varisais through advanced krithis and manodharma (improvisation), along with dedicated devotional repertoire.

Lessons are organized into three levels — Sa (Beginner), Ga (Intermediate), and Pa (Advanced) — each building on the concepts and repertoire of the last.

In-person classes are held on Long Island, NY. Students anywhere else in the world can join our online classes.

Online lessons are held one-on-one over video call, at a schedule that works for you, with the same structured curriculum used in our in-person classes.

All lessons are taught personally by Smt. Divya Meena Guhan, a third-generation Carnatic musician and recipient of the "Sangeeta Vidwath Bhooshan" title.

A stable internet connection, a laptop or tablet with a working camera and microphone, and a quiet space to practice are all you need to get started.

No prior knowledge of an Indian language is required. Lyrics and meanings are explained clearly in English as part of every lesson.
